The visual language of the legacy app, recorded so the rebuild can reproduce it without redesigning (CLAUDE.md: "reuse the legacy colour theme and component look"). Written from source on 2026-10-26 (P0.5). Screenshots of the result: `docs/baseline/` (P0.6–P0.8).

Sources, with `LEGACY/` = `C:/Users/lukam/Coding Projects/MobileDev/Ethical-App`:

## Character in one paragraph

A **light, warm, flat** Material-3 palette generated by Google Stitch:
- warm off-white surfaces (`#fcf9f4`);
- a very dark forest green as the brand colour (`#173809`);
- a terracotta secondary (`#94492c`);
- an olive tertiary.

Cards are white (`surfaceContainerLowest`) on the warm surface, with 16 px corners and **no shadows**. Headlines are large, very heavy (weight 900) and green, with slight negative tracking. Small labels are uppercase with wide tracking. Pills and buttons are fully rounded. Icons are Material Community Icons. There is no dark mode for screens.

### Tint convention

Grade and rating badges use their colour at **~9% alpha** for the background: hex colour + `"18"`. The text is in the full colour. Category pills on images use `primaryFixed + "40"` (25%).

## Fonts

- **No custom fonts are loaded.** There is no `useFonts`, no font assets, and `expo-font` is unused in code. Every screen renders in the **platform system font**: Roboto on Android, San Francisco on iOS, and the browser default sans on web, with `global.css` putting `Spline Sans, Inter` first in `--font-display` but loading neither.
- The look comes from **weight and size**, not typeface: 900 for wordmark and hero titles, 700 for card titles, 600 for labels and buttons, 500 for subtitles.
- The Stitch designs (`stitch-designs/*.html`) specify **Epilogue** (headlines, 400–900) and **Public Sans** (body and labels, 300–700) from Google Fonts. The legacy app never adopted them.
- **Rebuild default:** match what legacy actually renders (system font + the weights below). Loading Epilogue/Public Sans to match Stitch would be a design change; it needs an owner decision, so add it to a later phase rather than doing it silently.
- `global.css` (web only) defines `--font-display` (Spline Sans, Inter, ui-sans-serif, system-ui, …), `--font-mono`, `--font-rounded` and `--font-serif`.

## Spacing

There is no spacing scale in use (`Spacing` is unused); values are literal but consistent:
- **Screen gutter: 24 px** horizontal, used everywhere (`paddingHorizontal: 24`, 26 uses; cards use `marginHorizontal: 24`).
- Card padding 12–18 (rows 14 × 12–14; spotlight 18; scan sheet 24; education card 32).
- Gaps 6–16. Section spacing 24–40 (`marginBottom` 24 under cards and heroes, 40 between scan sections).
- Top of tab screens: `insets.top + 16`. Bottom: `96 + insets.bottom` to clear the floating tab bar.
- Lists: 8 px between rows (`marginBottom: 8`), 12 between story cards.

## Elevation and shadows

**Flat design.** Cards have no shadow or elevation; separation comes from white cards on the warm `surface`. Only two shadows exist:
- **Tab bar:** `shadowColor onSurface`, offset (0, -8), opacity 0.06, radius 24, `elevation: 0` (so iOS/web only).
- **Scanner laser:** `#7CFC00` glow, radius 6, opacity 0.9.

- 4 tabs: Impact (leaf), Scan (barcode-scan), Search (magnify), History (history).

### Stack headers (`src/app/_layout.tsx`)
- Native stack header shown on detail and secondary screens: background `surface`, tint (back arrow + title) `primary`, back title "Back".
- Titles: Product Details, Company Details, Receipt Details, Scan Receipt, Receipt Results, Impact Stories, Settings, Privacy, Help & Support, About, Profile.

- **Profile:** modal presentation.

### Status-bar scrim (`StatusBarScrim`)
- Used on Home, where content scrolls under a translucent status bar.
- An absolute band at the top: a solid `surface` band the height of the top inset, then a 12 px gradient from `surface` to transparent. zIndex 10, no pointer events.
- Rendered only when `insets.top > 0`. Prevents headlines from being cut by the camera hole.

### Navigation theme
React Navigation theme colours: background/card = `surface`, text = `onSurface`, border = `outlineVariant`, primary = `primary`. In OS dark mode the stock `DarkTheme` is used for navigator chrome only; screens stay light.

### Motion
- `FadeInDown` entrances (Reanimated), 300–350 ms, staggered 40–80 ms per item.
- `PressableScale` spring (0.97) on large cards.
- `LayoutAnimation` on the FAQ accordion.
- Haptics on scan, request and success/error.

## Admin portal look (`admin/src/index.css`)

The admin has its **own, separate palette**. It does not use the app's M3 tokens.
- **Light, quiet, dense:** `--bg #f7f8f7`; surfaces `#ffffff` / `#f1f4f2` / `#e8edea`; borders `#e3e8e5` / `#cfd7d2`; text `#171d1a` / dim `#5f6b65` / faint `#8a948e`.
- **Single green accent** `--accent #157a4e` (strong `#0f5e3c`, soft `#e2f2ea`, border `#bfe0d0`). This is a brighter, bluer green than the app's `#173809`.
- **Status colours:** danger `#b3362a` / soft `#fbe9e7`; warning `#996515` / soft `#fcf3e1`; info `#33538f` / soft `#e8eef9`. Rating colours `--good` = accent, `--mixed` = warning, `--poor` = danger.
